# PersonalOS Ultra Automation privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026.

This is a personal automation utility operated by its Google account owner. It is not a public service and has no third-party signup.

## Access, purposes and destinations

The knowledge publisher requests Google Drive `drive.file` access. It creates and updates explicitly selected research copies and verifies their contents through the Google Docs API. This permission covers app-created files or files explicitly selected by the owner; it does not request access to the entire Drive. The initial publishing collection contains already-public product documentation. Original sources remain authoritative; the research copies are not a lossless backup.

The video uploader requests YouTube upload access and read-only channel/video access. It verifies the destination channel and uploads reviewed product-video assets as private drafts, with subscriber notifications disabled. The underlying YouTube permission can technically upload public videos, but the recurring worker does not offer a public publishing operation. Public release is a separate deliberate step. Uploaded assets may include clearly identified generated illustrative footage alongside real product captures.

Bounded Google Cloud compute processes explicitly selected public or synthetic inputs. No production application credentials or private knowledge corpus are required for the initial jobs. Additional sources or changed data practices require an explicit scope decision.

## Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are kept in access-restricted files under the owner's control and protected by the owner's credential-management and encrypted-backup procedures. Google stores the selected research documents, uploaded video drafts and compute artifacts under its own terms and privacy policies. This documentation website does not receive those files or credentials. GitHub hosts this documentation and may process normal website access information under its privacy statement.

The utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, share it with an independent developer, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Use and transfer of Google user data adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Retention and controls

The owner controls document, video and compute-artifact retention. Revoking an app connection in Google Account settings stops its future access, but does not itself delete existing documents, videos or local recovery material. The owner can stop the scheduled jobs and separately remove their created files or video drafts. Research viewers and generated summaries are complementary views, not authoritative recovery archives.

The owner controls the support email displayed in Google's OAuth consent screen. Documentation corrections can be made through the source repository. This policy will be updated if data practices change.
